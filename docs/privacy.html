App identity

Police B1 Test is an independent educational exam preparation app, not an official Haryana Police or government service and not endorsed by any government authority.

Study data

Question responses, bookmarks, personal notes, revision progress and test history are saved locally on the user's device. No developer-owned server or login is used to upload this information. Users may export or erase progress in the app's Settings. Uninstalling normally deletes local data, subject to device backup settings.

Advertising

The app integrates Google AdMob. When permitted and able to load ads, Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's advertising partners may process device and app information, the Android advertising ID (if available), IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, ad interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, security and other disclosed purposes. The app publisher does not sell saved answer histories to advertisers.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosures.

Consent and controls

Where required, the Google User Messaging Platform presents consent choices before advertising requests. Applicable privacy options may be reopened in app Settings. Studying works without ads if consent is unavailable or advertising fails to load. Users can manage their advertising ID in Android settings.

Service providers and transfers

Google and its authorized ad partners may process ad-related data on servers outside a user's home country. Their published privacy practices govern this processing.

Audience

This app is intended for adult exam candidates and is not directed to children.
